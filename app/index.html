Roadmap to the Platinum

Difficulty 4/10 · About 200 hours · 1 playthrough · 1 potentially missable trophy · No mission replay, no New Game Plus. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so play clean. Keep 15 manual saves plus the autosave: save at the start and end of each chapter (10 saves) and rotate the other 5 during cleanup.

  1. Step 1 — Play the story (60–80 hours)

    Play through all 5 chapters and finish the final mission. Along the way, do the optional missions, scan vehicles and study animals with binoculars — that progress counts later. Do the Chapter 1 meditation at Jason’s home right after the mission Down to Earth: it may be the list’s only missable moment.

  2. Step 2 — 100% Completion checklist (about 100 hours)

    After the story, open Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion. It needs 100 optional missions (there are 200–300+), 200 of 277 base vehicle models scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied, 300 stunts, 70 of 80 challenges, and every robbery, underwater discovery, shooting range, drone race and Bret quest.

  3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup (about 10 hours)

    Finish the eight stranger mission strands and sweep up the remaining miscellaneous trophies — activities, collectibles and the partner robberies — with the tracker below.
