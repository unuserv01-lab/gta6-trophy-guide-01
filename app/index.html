    Roadmap to the Platinum

    Difficulty 4/10 · About 200 hours · 1 playthrough · 1 potentially missable trophy · 0 online trophies · No mission replay, no New Game Plus. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so play clean.

    1. Step 1 · 60–80 hours

      Play the story

      Play through all 5 chapters and finish the final mission, Starting From Zero. Along the way, do the optional missions, scan vehicles and study animals with binoculars — that progress counts later. Do the Chapter 1 meditation at Jason’s home right after the mission Down to Earth: it may be the list’s only missable moment.

      Earns: all 13 story trophies + Positive Mental Attitude

    2. Step 2 · about 100 hours

      100% Completion checklist

      After the story, open Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion. It needs 100 optional missions (there are 200–300+), 200 of 277 base vehicle models scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied, 300 stunts, 70 of 80 challenges, and every robbery, underwater discovery, shooting range, drone race and Bret quest. The checklist is closer to 60% true completion — forgiving, but long.

      Earns: A Long Road (gold) + Cunning Stunts, The Shootist, Top Fun, One Last Thing, Whip It Good, The Goodtime State, Maximum Withdrawal, Pest Control, Dime on the Dollar

    3. Step 3 · about 10 hours

      Strangers & Freaks and cleanup

      Finish the eight stranger mission strands and sweep up the remaining miscellaneous trophies — activities, collectibles and the partner robberies — with the tracker. Four trophies had no confirmed method at release; this guide marks them honestly instead of inventing one.

      Earns: Strangers & Freaks (silver) + the side-quest and miscellaneous trophies

    Save advice: 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave. Keep a save at the start and end of each chapter (10 saves) and rotate the other 5 during cleanup — there is no mission replay to fall back on.

    Roadmap built from the official trophy descriptions. Source: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap

    Missable trophies

    Only 1 trophy in the whole list is potentially missable. Everything else can be earned after the story in free-roam.

    Bronze · Missable

    Positive Mental Attitude

    Meditate at every spot across Leonida.

    There are 18 meditation spots at 10 locations, tracked at Progress, then Collections, then Exploration, then Meditations. Doing a spot with both Jason and Lucia counts double (+2). The risky one is the first meditation, in Chapter 1 at Jason’s home: after the mission Down to Earth, find Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony when she talks about meditating, and choose Accept. It is not yet proven whether that one is required for the trophy — the 100% checklist only needs 15 of 18 — but do it while you can, because it never comes back.

    Source: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap

    Everything else is safe

    Quest givers who vanish for a while — Kenton between Chapters 1 and 2–3, for example — always come back. Optional missions that disappear do not matter for trophies: there are far more than the 100 the checklist needs. Strangers & Freaks, fences, shops and gang operations all stay available in free-roam after the story.

    Source: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap
